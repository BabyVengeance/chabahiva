Benefits for Investors:

  • Strategy-aligned social investments

    Direct alignment with your corporate CSI mandate and national health priorities.

  • Quantifiable reporting on ESG investments

    Verifiable field data, quarterly monitoring, and audit-grade performance metrics.

  • Decreased operational burden

    We conduct on-the-ground due diligence, vetting, compliance tracking, and dispute management.

  • Reduced risk of misappropriation of investments

    Independent High Court fiduciary oversight ensuring transparent fund management.

  • Ethical practices focused on the sustainable strengthening of communities

    Building durable resilience rather than short-term dependency.

Grassroots Practitioners

Implementing Partners

Sustainable income generation, fund management, support and institutional strengthening for community organisations.

Benefits for Non-Profit Organisations:

  • Increased capacity and resources

    Increased resources available to respond to the HIV/Aids epidemic in a sustainable manner.

  • Increased organisational strength

    Governance coaching, management accounts guidance, and compliance readiness.

  • Isolation replaced by collaboration

    Direct linkage to municipal health districts, clinical departments, and peer networks.

  • Long term financial resilience

    Advisory services to incubate sustainable income streams beyond single ad-hoc donors.

  • Recognisable independent, trusted identity

    Affiliation with an established, audited High Court trust network.

Open Call for Programme Grants:

Grants up to ZAR 250,000 for 6 months available for registered South African NPOs and CBOs (2-5 years operating history).
