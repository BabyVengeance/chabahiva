PAIA Section 51 Manual

Prepared in accordance with Section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act (Act 2 of 2000).

1. Purpose of the PAIA Manual

This Manual is compiled in terms of Section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act, 2000 (Act No. 2 of 2000) ("PAIA") for the CHABAHIVA Trust. The purpose of this Manual is to guide requests for access to records of the Trust and to provide guidance on the grounds for refusal, request procedure, and prescribed statutory fees.

4. Schedule of Records Available Without Formal Request

The following categories of records are automatically available to the public without requiring a formal request under PAIA:

  • Public information brochures and website content.
  • Terms of Reference for Open Calls for Funding Applications.
  • Published educational materials featuring CT Giraffe.
  • Statutory registration particulars (High Court registration and PBO status).

5. Records Requiring Formal Request (Form 2)

Access to the following records must be formally requested via the prescribed Form 2:

  • Trust deed, founding constitution, and amendments.
  • Minutes of the Board of Trustees meetings.
  • Financial accounts, donor disbursement registers, and statutory audit reports.
  • Grant evaluation scorecards and institutional due diligence dossiers.
  • Personnel contracts and administrative correspondence.

6. Grounds for Refusal of Access

The CHABAHIVA Trust may lawfully refuse requests for access to records in accordance with Chapter 4 of PAIA, including the mandatory protection of personal information of third parties, commercial trade secrets, confidential fiduciary evaluations, and legal professional privilege.
