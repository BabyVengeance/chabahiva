Open Call for Funding Applications for HIV/AIDS Programmes

Funding Cycle: 6 Months High-Impact Community Intervention

Grant Allocation Up to ZAR 250,000

About Us: CHABAHIVA Trust is pleased to announce an open call for funding applications from Nonprofit Organisations (NPOs) and or Community-Based Organisations (CBOs) operating within South Africa. This funding opportunity is designed to support impactful HIV/AIDS programmes that contribute to sustainable community development. This funding call is open exclusively to NPOs and CBOs based and operating in Gauteng, North West, Eastern Cape and KwaZulu Natal.

Please note: CHABAHIVA Trust does not fund religious content or activities. All funded initiatives must align with our mission to combat the HIV/AIDS crisis through evidence-based programming and organisational strengthening.

Key Details

Application Closing Date 25 July 2025
Funding Amount Up to ZAR 250,000
Funding Period 6 months
Eligible Provinces Gauteng, North West, Eastern Cape, Mpumalanga, KwaZulu-Natal

Eligibility Criteria

Organisations must meet the following requirements:

Programme Focus Areas:
  • HIV prevention and care programmes.
  • Prevention and/or treatment programmes targeting key populations.
  • Community Health Workers initiatives.
Operational Requirements:
  • Registered NPO in South Africa, with preference to Youth-led organisations responding to health and wellness initiatives.
  • Actively operating in Gauteng, North West, KwaZulu Natal or Eastern Cape.
  • Organisation age: 2 to 5 years.
  • Clear programme plan for the funding period.
  • Commitment to advancing Sustainable Development Goal 3 (Good Health and Wellbeing).

Mandatory Supporting Documents

All applicants must submit seven statutory and governance verification documents in order to be considered:

Financial & Operating Disclosures
  • 1. Latest Annual Report
  • 2. Latest Audited Financial Statements
  • 3. Management Accounts (01 July 2024 - 30 June 2025)
Statutory Registration & Governance
  • 4. Valid NPO Registration Certificate
  • 5. Valid Tax Clearance Certificate
  • 6. List of Directors (with a 2-page CV for each)
  • 7. Organisation's Constitution
