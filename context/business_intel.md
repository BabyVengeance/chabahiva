# BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE - CHABAHIVA TRUST

## Organization Overview
- Legal Entity: CHABAHIVA Trust (Church Association for Building Assets for HIV and AIDS)
- Classification: South African Non-Profit Social Investor and Healthcare Intermediary
- Mission: Bridging capital allocators and frontline community health organizations to reduce HIV/AIDS infections and support vulnerable communities across South Africa.
- Core Footprint: Mpumalanga, North West, Gauteng, and Western Cape provinces.
