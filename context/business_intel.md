## 2. Core Purpose & Operating Model
CHABAHIVA Trust is a specialized South African social investor bridging the critical gap between capital allocators (corporate ESG, private foundations, international development funds) and frontline community organisations (NPOs, CBOs) fighting HIV/AIDS.

### Why They Exist
- Frontline organisations fighting the ongoing HIV/AIDS crisis face severe donor fatigue, reduced global funding, and volatile single-donor dependency.
- Corporate and international investors hesitate to allocate directly to grassroots NPOs due to due-diligence burdens, compliance complexity, and risk of fund misappropriation.
- CHABAHIVA Trust acts as the verified, fiduciary bridge: channeling capital with rigorous governance, monitoring & evaluation (M&E), and SROI tracking.
- Guided by UNAIDS 95-95-95 targets: 95% diagnosed, 95% on antiretroviral therapy (ART), 95% virally suppressed.

## 4. The Four Core Innovations
1. **Develop Interventions:**
   - Moves away from rigid, cookie-cutter interventions.
   - Deploys localized, structured peer educator systems and community health worker frameworks tailored to the exact socio-demographic realities of vulnerable populations.
2. **Collaborative Network:**
   - Unites government departments (Department of Social Development, Department of Health), corporate sponsors, faith-based networks, and community service providers.
   - Eliminates siloed efforts and aligns resources directly to community hotspots.
3. **Income Generation Advisory:**
   - Solves the single-donor dependency crisis for grassroots NPOs.
   - Provides strategic advisory on creating diversified revenue streams, social enterprise models, and sustainable financial resilience.
4. **Fund Management & Due Diligence:**
   - Fiduciary protection for investors: complete audit readiness, transparent reporting, statutory compliance, and quantifiable ESG impact metrics.
   - Guidance for NPOs to meet institutional investor standards without bureaucratic paralysis.

## 5. Funding Calls & Grantmaking Reality
- **Grants Window:** Competitive open calls for South African NPOs and CBOs.
- **Typical Allocation:** Up to ZAR 250,000 per organisation for 6-month high-impact cycles.
- **Target Focus:** HIV prevention and treatment for key populations, community health worker networks, and youth-led organisations.
- **Priority Geographic Footprint:**
  - Gauteng (Hammanskraal, Westonaria, Pretoria, Randburg)
  - Western Cape (Stellenbosch, Wellington, De Doorns)
  - Mpumalanga (Piet Retief)
  - North West (Mahikeng, Rustenburg)
  - Eastern Cape (Indwe)
- **Strict Policy:** Does **NOT** fund religious content or sectarian proselytizing; all allocations require evidence-based public health programming and measurable SDG 3 alignment.
